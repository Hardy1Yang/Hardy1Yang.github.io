Teaching

Statistics, measurement, and judgment

Eight semesters as a teaching assistant for NTU Psychology's quantitative sequence, two faculty workshops on AI in teaching, an interactive teaching tool, and three proposed courses.

← Back to home

01

Teaching philosophy

  1. Start from the research question, not the formula. Each method is introduced only when it answers a problem students already recognize — a between-subjects design, a scale that needs validation, a latent trait to be estimated.
  2. Pair theory with reproducible computation. Every method is implemented in R alongside its derivation; students submit analyses as reproducible scripts, so the link between an equation and its output is something they build.
  3. Treat AI tools as objects of critical analysis. Students compare AI-generated analyses with their own, locate where the statistical reasoning goes wrong, and say why. The goal is judgment, not prohibition.
